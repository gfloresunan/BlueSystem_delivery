## 1. Implementation Inspection

In `flutter_client/lib/platform/gps/gps_adapter.dart`:
- `PlatformGpsAdapter` implements the canonical `ILocationService` interface:
  - `checkLocationPermission()`: Evaluates `Geolocator.checkPermission()`.
  - `requestLocationPermission()`: Requests location permission interactively.
  - `getCurrentDeviceLocation()`: Fetches position with `LocationAccuracy.high` and a strict 10-second timeout.
  - `livePositionStream`: Stream with `LocationAccuracy.high` and `distanceFilter: 10` (10 meters debounce filter) to prevent excessive telemetry updates and battery drain.

---

## 2. ADR-015 Compliance

ADR-015 freezes the X→Y Location Architecture:
1. **Zero Places SDK:** Prohibits Google Places SDK inclusion. The Flutter GPS adapter directly samples hardware GPS via Geolocator.
2. **Deterministic Distance:** Telemetry coordinates (`LocationPoint`) match canonical lat/long precision.
3. **Fail-Closed Permission Handling:** If permissions are denied, throws `PlatformCapabilityException` rather than returning dummy or mock coordinates.
