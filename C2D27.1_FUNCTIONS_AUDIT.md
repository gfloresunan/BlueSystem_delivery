## 2. Forensic Discovery & Controlled Hardening

During the pre-flight forensic audit, inspection of `functions/src/callables/identity.ts` revealed that `executeSwitchActiveTenantContext` enforces a strict zero-trust security gate (`validateSwitchContextInput`):
- Passing `tenantId`, `brandId`, `businessId`, or `branchId` directly causes an immediate `invalid-argument` exception:
  `"Violación de Seguridad: Parámetro prohibido '${forbidden}' detectado en la solicitud."`
- The backend mandates that the client provide only `targetMembershipId`, resolving all tenant and role claims authoritatively on the server.
- **Surgical Hardening Applied:** `CloudFunctionsService.switchTenantContext` was updated to accept `required String targetMembershipId` and send `{ 'targetMembershipId': targetMembershipId }`, eliminating this security violation before physical testing.
