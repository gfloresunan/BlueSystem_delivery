## 1. Auth Contract & Service Architecture

In `flutter_client/lib/data/services/firebase_auth_service.dart`:
- `FirebaseAuthService` implements `IAuthService`:
  - `authStateChanges`: Stream converting Firebase `User` into `UserProfileEntity`.
  - `getCurrentUser()`: Hydrates user profile from `/users/{uid}` in Firestore.
  - `getCustomClaims()`: Resolves `IdTokenResult` claims into `CanonicalCustomClaimsV3`.
  - `signInWithEmailPassword(email, password)`: Authenticates against Firebase Auth and records audit log.
  - `signOut()`: Logs out and emits `LOGOUT_SUCCESS` audit event.
  - `refreshIdToken()`: Forces token refresh via `getIdTokenResult(true)`.

---

## 2. Inviolable Governance Rule: Non-Issuance Invariant

- **Flutter is a Pure Claims Consumer:**
  - Flutter deserializes `role`, `tenantId`, `brandId`, `orgId`, `businessId`, `branchId`, `status`, and `eiamVer` from the Firebase JWT token.
  - Flutter CANNOT emit, mutate, or forge custom claims.
  - Flutter CANNOT elevate user privileges or bypass backend role hierarchies.
  - All role changes and tenant context switching are performed exclusively by backend Cloud Functions (`switchActiveTenantContext`, `setUserClaims`).
